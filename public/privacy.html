Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Student Explorer helps students in Bengaluru find affordable places and plan group outings. This page explains, in plain words, what information the site uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, college email and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
  • What you add: outings and expenses you log, reviews and comments, places you add (name, address, map pin, photos you upload), favourites and trip plans.
  • Feedback: the message, optional photo and optional reply email you send through the Feedback button. Only the site admin can read it.
  • Verify visit: when you tap "Verify visit", your device's location at that moment is sent to us and saved with the verified visit, so fake check-ins can be prevented.
  • Anonymous usage counts: how many times a place was shown, opened or had directions tapped. These counts are not linked to you.

Your location

The site asks your browser for permission before using your location. It is used to sort places by distance and estimate fares. Your location is not stored for browsing; it is stored only when you use "Verify visit" (see above). You can say no and choose your area manually instead.

Sponsored places

Some places are promoted by their owners. Promoted places are always labelled "Sponsored". Payment never changes ratings or reviews.

Who can see your information

  • Your name may be shown next to reviews, comments and places you add.
  • Your email, password and feedback are visible only to you and, where needed, the site admin.
  • We do not sell your information. We do not show advertisements based on your personal data.

Other services

Directions and map previews open in Google Maps, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We use a sign-in cookie to keep you logged in, and your browser's storage for settings such as dark mode and your last recent searches. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own outings, reviews and places from the app.
  • To ask for your account and data to be deleted, or to ask a question about this policy, use the Feedback button in the app and choose "Something else". Please include your account email.

Children

The site is meant for college students. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.

This policy describes how the site works today and is written in plain language. It is not legal advice.
